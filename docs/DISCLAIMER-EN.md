# Mmolio watch disclaimer

**English** · [Čeština](DISCLAIMER-CS.md)

Mmolio is not a medical device. It is a supplemental display of readings obtained
by your sensor and phone application. It does not measure glucose or make treatment
recommendations. Do not base insulin dosing, food decisions or assessment of your
condition on Mmolio; use your usual official sensor application and glucose meter.

Keep the official sensor application's alarms enabled. Mmolio's watch app, watch
face and data field do not provide glucose alarms. Values may be wrong, delayed,
missing or old. Check the measurement age; a recent transfer is not a new measurement.
The watch marks stale data as STALE and future measurement times as CHECK TIME.

see the project's [full disclaimer](DISCLAIMER.md).
